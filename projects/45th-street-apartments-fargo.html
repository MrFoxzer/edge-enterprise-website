CASE STUDY · FARGO, ND

45th Street Apartments

Rough carpentry labor on a market-rate apartment development in Fargo's Anderson Park neighborhood. GC: Meridian Commercial Construction.

THE WORK

What Edge Is Framing

Edge Enterprise holds the rough carpentry labor subcontract from Meridian Commercial Construction on the 45th Street Apartments. The scope covers panelized walls, floor and roof trusses, sheathing, backing, Tyvek housewrap, windows, patio doors, A/C sleeves, stair assemblies and draft-stop doors.

City of Fargo planning records describe each apartment building as four stories: a precast concrete parking garage at grade on the main floor, with wood-framed construction above. The PUD master plan approved by the City of Fargo shows five four-story apartment buildings with main-level parking, plus one single-story commercial building along 45th Street South.

The apartment buildings are long and repetitive (the architect's facade calculations list a 240-foot front facade), so the work is a repeating wall, truss and sheathing sequence, floor after floor.

Public record

Building descriptions on this page come from City of Fargo records: Planning Commission packets of May 6, 2025 (PDF, 27 MB) (PUD master plan and narrative) and April 7, 2026 (PDF, 61 MB) (final plan and elevations), and the City Commission minutes of November 10, 2025 (master plan approval).
