LOCAL MARKET

Waukesha Construction Market

Waukesha is a strong regional market in Waukesha County — big enough for steady multi-family and commercial work, small enough that reputation still travels fast between builders and inspectors. Edge Enterprise LLC serves Waukesha and the wider Waukesha County area with full-scope structural framing — commercial, multi-family, custom residential, and finished carpentry — backed by 25+ years in the trade and more than 1,000 framed units across the Upper Midwest.

Waukesha sits roughly 8 hours from our Long Prairie HQ. For multi-week framing packages we stage crews locally, so drive time never becomes your schedule's problem. Wisconsin work falls under the Wisconsin Commercial Building Code and the Uniform Dwelling Code (UDC) for residential — a code framework with its own quirks that we know well. Typical design values around Waukesha — ground snow loads near 30–35 psf, design wind speeds of 105–115 mph, and frost depths around 48–60 in. — shape how we detail every frame; exact values are confirmed with the local building official before we cut the first plate.
