LOCAL MARKET

Wausau Construction Market

Wausau is a strong regional market in Marathon County — big enough for steady multi-family and commercial work, small enough that reputation still travels fast between builders and inspectors. Edge Enterprise LLC serves Wausau and the wider Marathon County area with full-scope structural framing — commercial, multi-family, custom residential, and finished carpentry — backed by 25+ years in the trade and more than 1,000 framed units across the Upper Midwest.

From our Long Prairie, MN headquarters, Wausau is about 5 hr 30 min away — close enough for our crews to mobilize quickly and staff the job for its full duration. Wisconsin work falls under the Wisconsin Commercial Building Code and the Uniform Dwelling Code (UDC) for residential — a code framework with its own quirks that we know well. Typical design values around Wausau — ground snow loads near 35–40 psf, design wind speeds of 105–115 mph, and frost depths around 48–60 in. — shape how we detail every frame; exact values are confirmed with the local building official before we cut the first plate.
