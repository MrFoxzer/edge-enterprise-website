LOCAL MARKET

Grand Forks Construction Market

Grand Forks is North Dakota's third-largest city with about 59,000 residents in the city and a metro of roughly 100,000 across the Red River with East Grand Forks, Minnesota. Two anchors drive the construction market here: the University of North Dakota (the state's flagship research university) and Grand Forks Air Force Base north of the city. Together they sustain a steady demand for student-adjacent multi-family, off-base military housing, and the periodic commercial build serving the downtown and 32nd Avenue corridors.

Grand Forks shares the standard 40 psf snow load and 115 mph design wind speed for the Red River Valley. Flood considerations matter more here than almost anywhere else we work — the 1997 flood permanently shaped the city's building code, elevation requirements, and floodplain-development rules. Any framing project below the FEMA-mapped 100-year elevation needs to integrate with the permit office's specific flood-resistant detailing, and we coordinate framing-to-FEMA-spec on every Grand Forks project. From Long Prairie that's about 4.5 hours up I-94 and US-2 — we crew on a long-week schedule similar to Bismarck, with overnight stays in the Grand Forks metro for the framing duration.

Flood-resistant detailing

Post-1997 Grand Forks code adds floodplain-elevation and flood-resistant framing requirements you don't see in the Twin Cities. Our crews build to that detailing as a baseline, not a change order.

02

UND-corridor multi-family fluency

Student-adjacent and off-base multi-family is the city's bread-and-butter. We've framed similar product across the Upper Midwest and understand the unit-mix and fire-rating choices that make sense in this market.

Cross-river GC coordination

Many Grand Forks projects involve construction managers operating on both the ND and MN sides of the Red River. We coordinate cleanly across both jurisdictions without dropping inspection signoffs.
