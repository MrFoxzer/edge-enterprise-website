LOCAL MARKET

Billings Construction Market

Billings is Montana's largest city with about 117,000 residents and a regional metro near 184,000. The construction market here has shifted noticeably over the past five years — what was historically an energy and agriculture market is now also absorbing significant in-migration from coastal markets, driving multi-family demand in West End and Heights neighborhoods that local crews haven't always been able to fully meet. Billings Clinic and St. Vincent Healthcare anchor a steady commercial build pipeline, and the downtown corridor has seen periodic mid-rise mixed-use investment.

Billings is Edge Enterprise's first Montana market following our service-area expansion, and we've structured our approach around full-project crew assignment rather than rotation — at 11 hours from Long Prairie HQ, weekly rotation simply doesn't pencil for the GC. Snow load is roughly 30 psf for the city itself (lighter than most Minnesota markets), with a 115 mph design wind speed. The local construction code adheres to the IBC with Montana state amendments, and Yellowstone County permits framing inspections through their own building department. We've ramped up our familiarity with Montana's specific energy-code requirements ahead of our first Billings projects.
