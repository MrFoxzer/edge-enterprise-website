LOCAL MARKET

Great Falls Construction Market

Great Falls is Montana's third-largest city with about 60,000 residents and a regional metro near 84,000, anchored by Malmstrom Air Force Base on the city's east side. The construction market here is more steady than spiking — ICBM-mission operations at Malmstrom, Benefis Health System, and the city's role as a regional service hub for north-central Montana keep multi-family and commercial building active without the in-migration pressure of Bozeman or Missoula. The downtown Civic Center and 10th Avenue South commercial corridors have seen the bulk of recent commercial reinvestment, with periodic multi-family along the western edge and Sun River corridor.

What makes Great Falls genuinely different from any Minnesota market is the Chinook wind. Warm Pacific air dropping over the Rocky Mountain Front creates wind events that locally exceed the city's 115 mph design wind speed by meaningful margins, and the rapid temperature and pressure swings during a Chinook stress framing assemblies in ways our Twin Cities work simply doesn't see. We frame for this with heavier roof-edge detailing, additional uplift connections, and Chinook-tolerant fastener schedules. From Long Prairie HQ Great Falls is about 13 hours west — fully-committed project crews, advance staging, and on-site supervisor presence for the duration are the norm.
