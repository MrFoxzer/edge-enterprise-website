Commercial Framing in Elk River — Questions We Hear

Do you frame commercial buildings in Elk River?

Yes — Edge Enterprise frames retail, office, hospitality, and light-industrial projects in Elk River and across Minnesota. We work as a framing subcontractor under general contractors as well as directly with owners on design-build work.

Can you handle both wood and steel framing on one project?

Yes. Many commercial buildings in Elk River mix dimensional lumber, engineered wood, and light-gauge steel. We self-perform both, which means one subcontract, one schedule, and one point of responsibility for the entire structure.

What size commercial projects do you take on?

Anything from small tenant build-outs to full ground-up commercial structures. Our sweet spot is wood and metal-stud framing packages in the $100K–$2M range, but we scale crews to the project.

Are you licensed and insured to work in Minnesota?

Yes — Edge Enterprise LLC is licensed, bonded, and fully insured for commercial work in Minnesota, and we carry workers' comp on every crew member on site.
