Wood Framing in Valley City — Questions We Hear

Do you do residential and commercial wood framing in Valley City?

Both. Edge Enterprise frames single-family homes, townhomes, multi-family buildings, and commercial wood structures in Valley City and the surrounding Barnes County area.

Can you frame through the winter?

Yes — our crews frame year-round across North Dakota. Cold-weather framing is standard practice for us: we plan material staging, snow removal, and dry-in sequencing so winter doesn't stall your schedule.

Do you install engineered wood products?

Yes. I-joists, LVL and PSL beams, glulam, and floor/roof trusses are on nearly every set of plans we frame. We install to the manufacturer's specifications and coordinate directly with the truss and EWP suppliers.
