Oak Creek Construction Market

Oak Creek is a strong regional market in Milwaukee County — big enough for steady multi-family and commercial work, small enough that reputation still travels fast between builders and inspectors. Edge Enterprise LLC serves Oak Creek and the wider Milwaukee County area with full-scope structural framing — commercial, multi-family, custom residential, and finished carpentry — backed by 25+ years in the trade and more than 1,000 framed units across the Upper Midwest.

Our Long Prairie, MN base puts Oak Creek about 8 hr 30 min out. We plan mobilization, material staging, and crew rotations around that from day one. Wisconsin work falls under the Wisconsin Commercial Building Code and the Uniform Dwelling Code (UDC) for residential — a code framework with its own quirks that we know well. Typical design values around Oak Creek — ground snow loads near 30–35 psf, design wind speeds of 105–115 mph, and frost depths around 48–60 in. — shape how we detail every frame; exact values are confirmed with the local building official before we cut the first plate.
