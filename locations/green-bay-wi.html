LOCAL MARKET

Green Bay Construction Market

Green Bay is Wisconsin's third-largest metro with about 107,000 residents inside the city limits and 328,000 across Brown County, anchored on the southern tip of the Bay of Green Bay. The construction market here is shaped by paper and manufacturing legacies, the Green Bay Packers / Lambeau Field commercial halo, and a steady multi-family demand serving Bellin Health, Aurora BayCare, and the city's role as a regional service hub for Northeast Wisconsin. The Titletown District redevelopment around Lambeau has driven a noticeable share of recent ground-up commercial and multi-family activity, and downtown Green Bay has seen ongoing reinvestment along Washington Street and in the Broadway District.

Green Bay sits in a 40 psf snow load zone — heavier than Madison or Milwaukee — with a 115 mph design wind speed that amplifies meaningfully along the bay shoreline. Lake-effect snow drift loads on multi-family with offset rooflines are non-trivial here, and we engineer truss bracing and roof sheathing accordingly. From Long Prairie HQ Green Bay is about 7 hours via I-94 and US-41 — at the upper edge of our weekly-rotation radius, so most Green Bay projects get a project-duration crew assignment with fewer rotations than a Madison or Twin Cities build would see.
