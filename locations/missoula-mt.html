LOCAL MARKET

Missoula Construction Market

Missoula is Montana's second-largest city by metro population with about 77,000 residents inside the city limits and a regional metro near 120,000. The construction market here is shaped by three forces simultaneously: the University of Montana drives sustained student-adjacent demand, Missoula's status as one of the most in-migration-saturated markets in the Mountain West has pushed median rents higher than most equivalent-size cities, and the city's tight valley topography genuinely limits buildable land. The result is a multi-family pipeline that punches well above its population weight, with infill and redevelopment doing more of the work than greenfield.

Snow load in the Missoula valley itself runs around 40 psf, similar to the Twin Cities, but mountain-channeled wind exposure can locally exceed the 115 mph design wind speed depending on the parcel — the canyon-channeling effect along the Bitterroot drainage is real and shapes shear-wall detailing in the southern neighborhoods. From Long Prairie HQ Missoula is about 12 hours west, so we structure projects here as fully-committed crew assignments with advance lumber and connector staging. Missoula County and the City of Missoula run their own building permits and inspections, and we've ramped up familiarity with their specific multi-family inspection sequence.
