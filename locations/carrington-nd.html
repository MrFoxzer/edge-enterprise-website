LOCAL MARKET

Carrington Construction Market

Carrington may be small, but small-town projects get our full crew and full attention — that's the Long Prairie way, and it's how Edge Enterprise was built. Edge Enterprise LLC serves Carrington and the wider Foster County area with full-scope structural framing — commercial, multi-family, custom residential, and finished carpentry — backed by 25+ years in the trade and more than 1,000 framed units across the Upper Midwest.

Carrington sits roughly 4 hr 45 min from our Long Prairie HQ. For multi-week framing packages we stage crews locally, so drive time never becomes your schedule's problem. North Dakota builds run under the state-adopted IBC/IRC, with city building departments — and open-prairie wind exposure — driving the structural detailing. Typical design values around Carrington — ground snow loads near 30–40 psf, design wind speeds of 115–120 mph (open-prairie exposure), and frost depths around 60–80 in. — shape how we detail every frame; exact values are confirmed with the local building official before we cut the first plate.
