FAQ

Finished Carpentry in Superior — Questions We Hear

Do you offer finished carpentry as a standalone service in Superior?

Yes — while we frame many of the buildings we trim, we also contract finish carpentry packages on their own: doors, casing, base, rails, shelving, and specialty millwork across Superior and Wisconsin.

Do you handle large multi-family trim packages?

Yes. Multi-family finish work — hundreds of doors, miles of base, unit shelving and hardware — is one of our core scopes, and our production tracking keeps late-building units as clean as the first ones.

Can you match existing trim in an older home?

Usually, yes. Between knife-ground profiles from local millwork shops and careful stain matching, we can tie new work into existing trim so the transition disappears.

What finish materials do you install?

Paint-grade and stain-grade wood trim, MDF, PVC, prehung and slab doors, hardware, closet systems, stair parts, and custom built-ins.
