LOCAL MARKET

West Fargo Construction Market

West Fargo is North Dakota's fastest-growing city, having more than doubled in population over the past two decades to roughly 39,000 residents inside the Fargo metro of 260,000. The construction market here is dominated almost entirely by new construction — single-family in the Eagle Run, Maple Valley, and Brooks Harbor subdivisions, plus a sustained multi-family build-out along Sheyenne Street, 13th Avenue, and the Veterans Boulevard corridor. We've framed in West Fargo extensively, including ENVY Apartments and several other multi-family projects with Comeland Builders.

West Fargo shares Fargo's 40 psf snow load and 120 mph design wind speed, and the wind exposure is even more pronounced here than in central Fargo — there's almost no natural windbreak west of the Sheyenne Diversion, so shear-wall, hold-down, and diaphragm-tie detailing all get scrutinized hard. From Long Prairie HQ West Fargo is about 3 hours and 15 minutes via I-94, and many of our crews stage out of Horace, ND, which is roughly 12 minutes south — that adjacency keeps mobilization tight and lets supervisors visit multiple Fargo-metro sites in a single day. The City of West Fargo runs its own building inspections separate from Cass County for projects inside city limits, and we maintain their preferred documentation for framing rough-ins.
